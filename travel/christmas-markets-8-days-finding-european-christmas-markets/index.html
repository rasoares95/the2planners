Christmas Markets

8 days finding European Christmas Markets
budapesthungaryviennaaustriavacationcity guideczech republicpraguechristmas1 week tripholidaysmarket

Welcome to one of the most beautiful trips that we made so far! 😍

We never thought that a trip could be as magical, as this was! All around the people were joyful and always smiling, you could hear people singing and the streets smelled like your grandma's house on Christmas Eve!

The only thing missing was the Snow! Oh dear Lord, if we got snow!!! But with snow or not this was an amazing trip! Be prepared and take good shoes and warm clothes! Because these countries are a little bit windy and they are really cold!
