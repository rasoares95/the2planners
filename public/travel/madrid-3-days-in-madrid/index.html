Madrid

3 days in Madrid
city guidevacationholidaysspainmadrid

Madrid is just over an hour's flight from Porto, so it is perfect for a long weekend: royal palaces, world-class museums, a huge park and tapas until late. We spent three days here in April 2019, flying in early in the morning and home late at night.

Tip: The prices and opening hours here are the ones we noted when we planned the trip. Check them before you go.

Day 1 - The historic center and sunset at the Temple of Debod

  • Gran Vía, Madrid's grand avenue of theatres and shops.
  • Puerta del Sol and Kilometer Zero, the center of Spain.
  • Plaza Mayor.
  • Mercado de San Miguel, for lunch.
  • The Royal Basilica of San Francisco el Grande (€3).
  • Almudena Cathedral: free, with a museum for €4. The museum is worth it, the dome less so.
  • The Royal Palace (€6), Plaza de Oriente and the Royal Theatre, all next to each other.
  • Sunset at the Temple of Debod, a real Egyptian temple, free.
  • Dinner and a night out in Chueca.

Day 2 - The Prado and El Retiro

  • The Neptune Fountain.
  • The Prado Museum. Free from Monday to Saturday from 6pm to 8pm, and on Sundays and holidays from 5pm to 7pm, and free for students up to 25.
  • El Retiro Park.
  • Puerta de Alcalá.
  • Plaza de Cibeles, with the Cibeles Palace and fountain.
  • Círculo de Bellas Artes, with a rooftop view over the city (€4).

Day 3 - Trains, El Rastro and Salamanca

  • The Railway Museum (€3).
  • San Isidro church, free.
  • El Rastro, Madrid's flea market, good for tapas. Watch out for pickpockets.
  • The Reina Sofía Museum, home of Picasso's Guernica. Free for students and on Sunday afternoons.
  • The Congress of Deputies, seen from outside.
  • The Salamanca district, for expensive shops, and the Mercado de la Paz, for a cheaper lunch.
