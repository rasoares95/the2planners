Fried Blue Whiting with Bean Salad

portuguesemain dishesfish

Fried Blue Whiting fish laden with lemon juice, will make your mouth water! It is a dish that can be accompanied by many delicacies, but nothing better than a good bean salad.

QuantitiesMaking more or less? We'll do the maths. Cooking times stay roughly the same.

Ingredients

Recipe

1. Place 6 eggs to cook in a pan with water for . After cooking, peel the eggs, slice them and add to the black beans and green beans (previously cooked in water and salt for about ).

2. Season with salt, olive oil, and vinegar and mix everything well. Take it to the fridge.

3. Remove the head of the fish and pass them through 3 whisked eggs and then flour.

4. With the oil very hot, fry the fish, and then while eating drizzle them with freshly squeezed lemon juice.
