French Tarte Tatin

frenchdesserts

When November comes, it's time to feel the Christmas spirit! Put some Christmas songs on at your place and let's go to the kitchen to make amazing dishes to be eaten near the fireplace!

Oh Lord, it's the most wonderful time of the year!

QuantitiesMaking more or less? We'll do the math. Cooking times stay roughly the same.

Ingredients

Recipe

1. Peel the apples and cut them into 4 slices each.

2. In a frying pan, place the sugar, ginger slices, anise stars, and butter.

3. Bring to medium heat and place the apples with the round faces down.

4. Without stirring the preparation, leave it in the pan for - it depends on the amount of water released by the apples while cooking - until it reaches a caramel color.

5. If the frying pan can go to the oven, cover it with the shortcrust pastry and bake at 180ºC | 356ºF.

6. If the frying pan cannot go to the oven, like ours, put it in a tart pan and then cover it with the shortcrust pastry and put it in the oven at 180ºC | 356ºF.

7. Leave in the oven for or until toasted.

8. Allow to cool for and it's time to plate it, be careful not to burn yourself on the hot caramel.

9. Let it cool down a bit and plate it with two scoops of ice cream and enjoy this delicacy!

10. It's so delicious!
