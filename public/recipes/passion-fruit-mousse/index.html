1. Whisk the passion fruit pulp (juice and seeds) with the condensed milk for a few minutes.

2. Pour the whipping cream into the mixing bowl and whisk at high speed until stiff peaks form.

3. Add the passion fruit mixture to the cream and stir until well mixed.
