1. In a glass, mix the warm water with sugar, olive oil, and instant dry yeast.

2. In a Stand Mixer place the dough hook and add the flour.

3. Turn on the mixer and slowly add the previous water preparation.

4. Let knead for until you get a nice, smooth dough.
