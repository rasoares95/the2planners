Homemade Seasoned Rustic French Fries

appetizersamerican
QuantitiesMaking more or less? We'll do the maths. Cooking times stay roughly the same.

Ingredients

Recipe

1. Wash the potatoes very well.

2. Cut the potatoes into sticks, keeping the potato skin.

3. Place the potatoes in a bowl and start seasoning.

4. Add salt, black pepper, paprika, and oregano.

5. Stir everything well.

6. Put vegetable oil to heat on a frying pan and when it's hot start frying your potatoes!

7. I promise they'll be the best french fries you've ever tasted!
