Portuguese Fried Codfish with Purée

main dishesportuguesefish

Codfish is very traditional in Portugal, so we do a lot of dishes with it! If you ever come to Portugal you can try it on almost every side of the country, in numerous ways!

QuantitiesMaking more or less? We'll do the math. Cooking times stay roughly the same.

Ingredients

Recipe

1. In a pan place the potatoes to be cooked with salt.

2. After cooking, remove the water, add the butter, milk, and egg yolk, and beat everything with an electric mixer to form a purée.

3. In a pan, place the four sliced onions to be sautéed with half the green and half the red pepper, diced, adding salt, olive oil, piri-piri, bay leaf, and tomato pulp.

4. Pass the four pieces of cod through flour and fry in very hot oil.

5. Next we are going to plate. On a platter, distribute the onion on the first base, followed by the cod slices and finally the puree.

6. To mark the location of the slices, you can add slices of pepper and olives to decorate.

7. Take to the oven for at 180 Celsius degrees | 356 Fahrenheit degrees.
