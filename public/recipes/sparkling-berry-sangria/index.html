Sparkling Berry Sangria

drinks

This is probably the drink we make most at home! This and caipirinha! You must do this as well! The berries are amazing with the sparkling wine and if you don't like berries you can swap them for other kinds of frozen fruit that you have!

You'll thank us later! We promise!

QuantitiesMaking more or less? We'll do the math. Cooking times stay roughly the same.

Ingredients

Recipe

1. In a 1L jar put the sugar, lemon juice, and cinnamon sticks. Mix everything and add the frozen berries.

2. Put 500mL of brut sparkling wine in the jar and let it rest in the refrigerator for .

3. Add the 500mL of any Soft Drink you like, we prefer Seven-Up Free, because it's low on sugar, it is colorless and does not add a strong flavor.

4. And that's it!!! Just fill a glass with ice and enjoy and do it again until the mood increases! 😂🤣
