Portuguese Meat or Ham and Cheese Rissoles

meatmain dishesportugueseappetizers
QuantitiesMaking more or less? We'll do the maths. Cooking times stay roughly the same.

Ingredients - Dough

1Kg Wheat Flour

1L Water

1 Pinch of Salt

1 Lemon Peel

150g Butter

Recipe - Dough

1. Put the water, salt, lemon peel, and butter in a large saucepan until boiling.

2. After boiling the water, put the flour in one go and stir until it forms a dough that comes out of the pan and immediately disconnects from the stove.

3. Form a ball with the dough and let it cool on a stone or fried bench and then, using a rolling pin, form small circles for the patties.

Ingredients - Meat Rissoles

Recipe - Meat Rissoles

1. In a pan, stew the beef and chicken with the chorizo, onion, and other spices.

2. After it is well cooked, put the meat in the chopper and grind everything and then fill the patties.

3. Pass the risotto over egg and breadcrumbs, then just fry or freeze.

Ingredients - Ham and Cheese Rissoles

Recipe - Ham and Cheese Rissoles

1. Place the cheese and ham in a chopper and grind well to create a paste to fill the patties.

2. Pass the risotto over egg and breadcrumbs, then just fry or freeze.
