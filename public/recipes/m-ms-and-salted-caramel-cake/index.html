M&Ms and Salted Caramel Cake

dessertscakechocolategluten free
QuantitiesMaking more or less? We'll do the maths. Cooking times stay roughly the same.

Ingredients

Recipe

1. In a Blender add the eggs and sugar and mix everything well.

2. Then add the milk, vanilla flavor, and vegetable oil until a homogeneous mixture is obtained.

3. Add the flour, salt, and baking powder.

4. After well mixed, divide the mixture into two bowls, and in one of them add the 180g of M&Ms, previously shredded.

5. Grease two forms with butter and bake the two cakes for at a temperature of 180ºC | 356ºF.

6. After removing the cakes from the oven, let them cool and cut the plain cake in half (the one without the M&Ms flour you made).

7. Time to prepare the filling and topping.

8. Break the chocolate into small pieces and place them in a bowl.

9. Bring the cream to boil and as soon as it starts to boil pour over the chocolate, stirring well until the chocolate is completely melted.

10. Divide the melted chocolate into two bowls and take one to the refrigerator.

11. Place half of the plain cake on a plate, brush with the melted chocolate, place the cake with the M&Ms flour, brush again with melted chocolate and then add the last half of the plain cake.

12. After the chocolate that went to the refrigerator is cold, with the help of a mixer, beat the chocolate and add the salted caramel until you get a mousse.

13. This mousse will be the topping for the whole cake!

14. In the end, just decorate with M&Ms as you like!
