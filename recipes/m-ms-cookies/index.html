M&Ms Cookies

dessertsbreakfastholidayschocolateamericancookies

Tonight we decided to make two trays of cookies for 2 friends of ours, that are dealing with some isolation time! 🤣 So it's time to feed the needy! Just joking, we love them and we love cooking cookies, so it's a win-win situation! 😍

We search for different recipes and different backgrounds, and now we present ours!

Hope you enjoy it!

QuantitiesMaking more or less? We'll do the maths. Cooking times stay roughly the same.

Ingredients

Recipe

1. Put 160gr of M&Ms in a plastic bag and with the help of a hammer break them off roughly.

2. In a mixer add softened butter with the sugars (the three types), mix them until a homogeneous mixture is created. Be careful to break all the brown sugar lumps during this process.

3. Add the egg and the vanilla essence.

4. Add the wheat flour, baking powder, baking soda and involve a few seconds until combined. Finally, add the crushed M&Ms and stir.

5. After everything is roughly involved, it is time to create the cookie balls.

6. Place a sheet of buttered parchment paper on a baking tray and place portions of the cookie dough.

7. Place 3 whole M&Ms in each serving to decorate.

8. Place in the oven, previously heated to 180ºC | 356ºF, for or until golden brown.

9. These cookies will be super chewy thanks to the molasses in the dark brown sugar.

10. Super easy to make and super delicious! Aren't they precious? 😍
