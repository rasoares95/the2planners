Focaccia al Pomodoro Recipe - Cherry Tomato Bread

Recipe

1. In a mug, put the sugar, the baker's yeast, and add the warm water, dissolve everything well and let it sit for .

2. In a bowl, add the flour and make a hole in the middle, gradually add the previous preparation and knead the dough with your hands.

3. Add the olive oil and salt and continue to knead for .

4. Cover the bowl with a cloth and let it rise for at room temperature.

5. One hour later, take a tray and place a sheet of buttered parchment paper and add the dough, let it rise for another .

6. After rising again, cut tomatoes in half and distribute them in the dough adding coarse salt.

7. Bake for at 200ºC | 392ºF

8. Cut the Focaccia into large pieces and make dipping sauces to accompany.
