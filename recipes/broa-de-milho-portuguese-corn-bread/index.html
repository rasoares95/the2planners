Broa de Milho - Portuguese Corn Bread

breadportugueseappetizerscake
QuantitiesMaking more or less? We'll do the maths. Cooking times stay roughly the same.

Ingredients

Recipe

1. Scald the corn flour in the boiling water, kneading well. Let it rest for .

2. In a bowl, dilute the yeast in 50mL of warm water and 1tbsp of sugar. Let stand for to activate the yeast. In another bowl, dilute the salt with the others 50mL of water.

3. Add wheat flour to the corn flour preparation, and add the water with yeast and the water with salt. Knead very well for a few minutes with the help of an electric mixer (), or by hand for about .

4. Cover the dough with a damp cloth and let it rise for in a warm place.

5. Transfer the ball-shaped dough to the oven tray. Sprinkle it with flour and make a cross in the center. Bake in a preheated hot oven for about at 220ºC | 430ºF.

6. After that time, remove the bread from the oven and you are ready!

TIP: If you want to eat it while still hot break the bread with your hands! If you want to slice it with a knife let it rest for . Spread some butter on it and be happy! 🧈 🥰
