Salmon Ceviche in Toasted Wraps

appetizersfish
QuantitiesMaking more or less? We'll do the maths. Cooking times stay roughly the same.

Ingredients - 2 Drinks

Recipe

1. Place the Wrap Bread in two bowls and take it to the oven for at 180ºC | 360ºF - These will be our ceviche bowls.

2. Cut the salmon fillet into equal cubes and add it to a large bowl.

3. Cut the avocado into cubes and add to the salmon, along with the juice of half a lemon, salt, and black pepper.

4. Slice the onion very finely and add to the prepared and break the orange into cubes to add.

5. Add the parsley and stir everything well and let it rest for .

6. Remove the wraps from the oven and let them cool! Then just plate!

7. Isn't it a super easy and delicious recipe?
