Portuguese Chorizo and Mozzarella Stuffed Bread

meatappetizersportuguesemain dishesbread

If you love cheese as much as we do, this recipe is so right for you! You can eat this at any time, but we prefer it when is still hot, this way the cheese will melt in your mouth, and the chorizo works perfectly here!

If you love species as much as we do, you can put some chili or add some spicy sausage as well.

Be delighted with this traditional recipe!

You can do a big one or small portions as we did! This recipe will make 6 portions.

QuantitiesMaking more or less? We'll do the maths. Cooking times stay roughly the same.

Recipe

1. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water, allowing to stand for . Then add the vegetable oil.

2. On a bench or in a bowl, place the flour and add the water with the yeast and oil, slowly.

3. Knead everything very well for .

4. Add salt and sugar and knead for another .

5. Place the dough in a bowl and cover with a cloth, allowing the dough to rise for in a warm place, about 30ºC | 86ºF.

6. Knead the bread again and with the help of a rolling pin, roll out the dough to fill the bread.

7. Cut portions of the dough and fill with chorizo, spicy sausage, and cheese.

8. To make it easier, you can wet the ends of the dough with water to better close the stuffed bread.

9. Place everything on a baking tray and let it rise for another in a warm place, around 30ºC | 86ºF.

10. Bake for at about 180ºC | 356ºF.
