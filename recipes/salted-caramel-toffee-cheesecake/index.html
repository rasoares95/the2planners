Do you remember trying those caramel sweets we ate when we were young? This is the memory that this cheesecake brings to me (Richie)!

Those were good times, I always had uncles that used to bring in their pockets this kind of candy!

You have to try this delicious cheesecake!

Recipe - Salted Caramel

1. Combine the sugar with cold water in a medium saucepan set over medium-high heat. Cook, without stirring, until the sugar has turned a deep amber hue.

2. Slowly add in the cream and continue simmering the mixture until it is smooth. Remove from heat, then whisk in the butter and salt, and let it cool.

3. Reserve it.

Recipe - Cheesecake

1. Mix the crushed wafer/cookies with the melted butter. Place this in a removable ring cake pan by pressing with the help of a spoon. Let it rest in the cold.

2. Beat the cream, and then add the cream cheese and vanilla flavor until creamy. Gradually add half of the salted caramel you made.
