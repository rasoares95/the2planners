Un Poco Loco Restaurant - Aveiro, Portugal

★★★★★
livingrestaurantportugalaveiro

If you ever go to Aveiro, in downtown, you have to go to Un Poco Loco Restaurant! This is a stop you need to make! This restaurant has the flavors on point and knows what they are doing! And don't get me started on those margaritas!

  • For Appetizers, we order nachos ( ai, ai, aiiiii), Nachos - Totopos with Melted Cheese and Jalapeños.

Review: This was the best dish of the entire meal (Margaritas aside)! I would go every weekend to Aveiro, just to eat this appetizer! They are hot, the cheese is melted in the oven, and the Jalapeños just adds the right amount of spice! I have to stop! My belly is begging again for this!

  • For Main Dish, we ordered two dishes for both of us to try on, Poco Loco Tacos - Veal Sirloin, Tortilla, Cheese, Caramelized Onion in Tequila, Onion Pickle and Chipotle Salsa. Accompanied by Lettuce Salad and we also ordered Tacos Paquito, Chicken, Tortilla, Lettuce, Salsa Roja, and "Salsa" Verde

Review: The Poco Loco Tacos, was sweet and juicy, lots of flavors there, and the Tacos Paquito had a strong flavor of chili and coriander. Both of them are really good, and it's just enough for two people!

  • For Dessert, we shared a Tres Leches Cake.

Review: It's good, it's a Tres Leches Cake, simple and spot-on. But we should have tried the coffee pannacotta as well.. but regrets make you come back to places like this! 🤭

The restaurant is very cozy, you can feel the Mexican spirit and the candles and saints all around make you feel in the mood!

Ps- I loved the Red neon light on display at the restaurant window!

Ps2 - Richie drank a Margarita Original and Andrew a Margarita Loca (this one was a Passion Fruit Margarita! What a nice and fresh surprise!)

Un Poco Loco, Aveiro - ★★★★★
